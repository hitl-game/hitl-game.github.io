Human in the Loop — visual directions

Same screen (layout A, Garage, day 9 of Loop #1) in four personalities. All four respect the anti-slop principle: the base workspace is sober, and only Metis wears gradients and ✨. Each direction is shown in its natural mode, plus the opposite mode and the Metis surface. Fonts are approximated with system fonts; the type choice comes later. The accent is never purple: purple belongs to Metis. Names ("Ramen Labs", "Bluewren", "Acme Corp") are placeholders, except Acme Corp from the cascade.

1 · Ink

Linear-like. Cool neutral greys, tight density, quiet, one blue accent. Feels like an engineering tool. Natural mode: dark. Type: Inter / numbers "JetBrains Mono". Radius 6px, base size 13px.
