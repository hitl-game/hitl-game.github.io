3 · What "Human in the Loop" is inside the fiction

The GDD says the header title and the tab title corrupt at Alignment 75 / 50 / 25, and the last stage arrives at Surpassed. So the name sits in the header — but as what? Each option shows the header at rest and the corruption stages as they would read in that position.

The suite (chat + dashboard) is called "Human in the Loop", the way Slack is called Slack. The player's startup is the workspace inside it.
