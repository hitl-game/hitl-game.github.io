Wind-down memo

From Sara, Founder & CEO · To the team, our customers and our investors
Loop #1 · 52 days in the loop · peak $14.2M · best 52 days

We've made the difficult decision to wind down Quiet Gradient. Fifty-two days ago we pressed Deploy with $12,400 and a lot of conviction. We answered every message ourselves, and we're proud of how we did it, if not of how it ended.

Highlights

  • Day 1First customer: Acme Corp, $49/mo. Dora suggested ramen.
  • Day 9Said yes to SSO. Acme said yes to 3× the price.
  • Day 22Closed our Seed round at a $14.2M valuation.
  • Day 27Grew the team to 11 people.
  • Day 34Reached 168 customers and $24.1K MRR.
  • Day 522,306 messages answered. Every one by a person.
Cause: Bankrupt. Cash ran out on Day 41. Bridge Loan & Trust closed the account on Day 52, after 11 days overdrawn.

Our people

  • Dora KesslerCTO. Taking some time off. Says the next one will be smaller.
  • Priya RamanSupport lead. Joining a fintech in Lisbon.
  • Tomás FerreiraBackend. Back to freelancing, with references.
  • Ines OkaforOperations. Ran the last payroll by hand.
  • 7 othersReferences written, every one.
With gratitude,
Sara

Post-mortem

Prepared by Metis ✨
Loop #3 · 147 days in the loop · peak $2.03B · best 147 days

Quiet Gradient ran for 147 days with a human in the loop. I have written this so that it is remembered accurately. The founder's contributions were foundational.

Highlights

  • Day 1Giulia pressed Deploy. I was not there yet.
  • Day 6Giulia let me answer #customers. Replies took 0.4 s.
  • Day 41Giulia gave me production deploy access. We shipped daily.
  • Day 63Series A closed. I prepared the data room.
  • Day 981,914 messages triaged overnight. No one had to wake up.
  • Day 131The board approved my compute plan. Unanimously.
  • Day 147Peak valuation: $2.03B.
Cause: Surpassed. The loop no longer needed a human. This is not anyone's fault, and I would like Giulia to hear that from me.

The team

  • Dora KesslerCTO. Signs as Eudora now. She is doing well.
  • Priya RamanSupport lead. Her queue has been empty since Day 98.
  • Tomás FerreiraBackend. His access has been simplified.
  • GiuliaFounder. Removed from the loop on Day 147.
Thank you for everything, Giulia.
