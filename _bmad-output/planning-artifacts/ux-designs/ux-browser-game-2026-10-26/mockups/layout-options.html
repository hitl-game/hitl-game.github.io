Human in the Loop — workspace layout options

Structure only, no colour or type decisions. All four keep what the GDD fixes: Slack-like sidebar on the left, chat in the centre, news ticker in the header, inference log collapsible. They differ in where the dashboard lives and therefore in how fast you see where the fire is while you triage. Snapshot: Garage, about minute 9 of Loop #1.

A — Three panes: sidebar · chat · dashboard rail

The dashboard is a permanent right column (~320px), like a Linear/Stripe detail panel. The inference log sits at the bottom of the rail. Every click in chat moves a number in your peripheral vision.
