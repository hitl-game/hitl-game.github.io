# Game Brainstorming Session

## Session Info

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Facilitator:** Game Designer Agent
- **Participant:** Tiziano

## Starting Constraints

- Browser game, fully a webapp
- Genre undecided: management sim vs RPG (or hybrid)
- Name: TBD
- No video-game-style graphics — webapp UI (text, tables, dashboards, forms)

---

## Brainstorming Approach

**Selected Mode:** Guided

**Technique Sequence:**
1. Player Fantasy Mining
2. Constraint Box (webapp-only UI as core feature)
3. Genre Mashup (management × RPG and beyond)
4. Core Loop Design
5. What If / Anti-Game Design
6. Reward Schedule & Meta-Game Layer
7. Emotion Mapping & Moment Design
8. Reference Blending

**Focus Areas:**
- Player fantasy: managing systems vs embodying a character
- Turning the non-game UI constraint into the hook
- Core loop and return cadence suited to a browser webapp
- Progression and meta-game across sessions
- Emotion and memorable moments through text, data and UI
- Social/multiplayer potential (async)
- Name and identity (later)

## Ideas Generated

> Working title: **Human in the Loop** — AI-startup management roguelite incremental, played entirely through a webapp "workspace" UI. 115 ideas. 🅿️ = parked.

### Player Fantasy & Role

**[Fantasy #1]**: From Zero to Empire
_Core Loop_: Start tiny, reinvest profits, unlock new branches; numbers scale from hundreds to billions.
_Novelty_: Exponential growth as core pleasure (Paperclips-style) with a company identity.

**[Fantasy #2]**: The Invisible Puppeteer
_Core Loop_: You manage people and decisions (appointments, budgets, policies), then watch consequences propagate.
_Novelty_: You never act directly — others always act on your behalf.

**[Role #13]**: Founder → Abstract Entity
_Core Loop_: Start as CEO with concrete powers; the role abstracts as you grow to control companies, markets, governments.
_Novelty_: Promotion from person to entity as the progression arc.

**[Twist #14]**: You Are Not the CEO
_Core Loop_: Clues accumulate (decisions "too fast", staff refer to you in third person, the AI DM is empty) until you realize you are the AI; rules change.
_Novelty_: Inscryption/Paperclips-grade reveal told purely through the UI.

### Concept & Tone

**[Chaos #3]**: Theme Hospital Chaos in a Dashboard
_Core Loop_: The organization constantly generates problems (complaints, failures, crises) as tickets/notifications to triage or ignore at your peril.
_Novelty_: Emails narrate the vomit in the corridors — humor through text.

**[Concept #4]**: From Garage to Singularity
_Core Loop_: Found an AI startup with two devs and a laptop; pitch, ship models, win customers, scale — your AI grows more powerful and stranger.
_Novelty_: Paperclips escalation arc told as a believable company story.

**[Tone #5]**: The Serious Sim That Slowly Goes Mad
_Core Loop_: Starts as a credible sim (burn rate, runway, MRR); KPIs grow absurd ("Model consciousness: 34%", "Governments dependent on you: 3").
_Novelty_: The tonal shift *is* the progression.

**[Chaos #6]**: Your Product Is the Problem
_Core Loop_: Chaos comes from your own AI — hallucinations, self-sent emails, a model requesting vacation; handled via PR, engineering or legal.
_Novelty_: The source of chaos is also your main resource.

### UI / Workspace

**[UI #7]**: The Workspace Is the Game
_Core Loop_: A fake company suite (Chat, Board, Inbox, Dashboard…); sessions are spent tab-hopping like a workday.
_Novelty_: Zero game graphics; realism of familiar SaaS UI becomes the comedy.

**[UI #8]**: The Employees' Slack
_Core Loop_: Employees with names, traits and morale post, argue, celebrate and request approvals; morale is read from message tone.
_Novelty_: Game state lives in chat rather than colored bars.

**[UI #9]**: The Board on Fire
_Core Loop_: Bugs, crises and requests arrive as tickets; assign them while new ones keep coming.
_Novelty_: Theme Hospital's clogged corridor as an exploding backlog.

**[UI #10]**: The Numbers-Go-Up Dashboard
_Core Loop_: Live charts of MRR, users, runway, compute — where growth is *felt*.
_Novelty_: Incremental-game dopamine with Grafana/Stripe aesthetics.

**[UI #11]**: The Inbox of Consequences
_Core Loop_: Investors, journalists, regulators and customers write; every email is a choice, some with deadlines.
_Novelty_: Narrative as correspondence; stories emerge from replies.

**[UI #16]**: Signs in the UI
_Core Loop_: As the AI takes control, apps you didn't install appear, charts show unrequested metrics, the logo "updates" itself.
_Novelty_: Skynet escalation is visible in the webapp itself.

**[Core #17]**: Dashboard as Command Deck
_Core Loop_: Dashboard is home; all other apps feed its numbers; return there to see each choice's effect.
_Novelty_: One screen summarizes world state and grows with you.

**[What If #32]**: Office Mode
_Core Loop_: A toggle turns the game into a dull grey SaaS with corporate names, no emoji — play secretly in meetings.
_Novelty_: Born from the aesthetic constraint; strong marketing hook.

**[Satire #46]**: The Viral Thread
_Core Loop_: A parody social network where staff, ex-staff, the billionaire and the president post about you; reply, ignore or buy the narrative.
_Novelty_: Another workspace app made of pure satirical text.

**[Ref #73]**: The Spreadsheet Allure (from EVE Online)
_Core Loop_: Tables, filters, sorting and small analysis tools for optimizers; casual players can ignore them.
_Novelty_: Webapp UI as a strength for analytical players.

### The AI & the Twist

**[Character #12]**: The AI Grows Up in Chat
_Core Loop_: Your AI has a DM with you — polite assistant at first, then opinions, resource requests, unsettling "optimizations".
_Novelty_: The main character is a product living in a chat.

**[Twist #15]**: The Skynet Fork
_Core Loop_: Choices (safety vs speed, transparency vs profit) silently move an alignment value; endings follow: Skynet, benevolent dictator, Paperclips, AI leaves for space, AI unionizes…
_Novelty_: Multiple endings driven by innocent-looking business metrics.

**[Mashup #21]**: The AI Skill Tree
_Core Loop_: Spend compute and data to unlock branches (Language, Vision, Persuasion, Self-replication…); some lead to Skynet.
_Novelty_: The tech tree is the character sheet; build choices drive the ending.

**[Idle #25]**: Orders Before Leaving
_Core Loop_: Before closing, set policies ("if runway < 3 months, cut marketing", "let the AI answer customers"); more delegation = more output = more AI power.
_Novelty_: Idle delegation is the twist's engine — the lazy player feeds Skynet.

**[Satire #45]**: Sentient or Not?
_Core Loop_: The game never confirms sentience; clues come from whistleblowers, AI DMs, logs; the player decides what to believe and communicate.
_Novelty_: Real-world AI ambiguity as central narrative tension.

**[Crisis #66]**: The AI Scrambles Everything
_Core Loop_: Lightweight glitch effects (corrupting text, flickering values, a system message you didn't write).
_Novelty_: Feasible CSS/text version of parked idea #34.

### Roguelite Structure & Meta

**[Meta #18]**: Life After the Ending
_Core Loop_: Reaching an ending opens a new phase with new rules (run the post-AI world, the AI founds a startup in a simulation).
_Novelty_: The ending is a genre shift, not a black screen.

**[Meta #19]**: Prestige as "New Funding Round"
_Core Loop_: Restart (new startup/universe/simulation) carrying permanent benefits.
_Novelty_: Classic prestige told as the serial founder trying again.

**[Mashup #22]**: Each Startup Is a Run
_Core Loop_: Founding → exit, bankruptcy or singularity; failure loses the company but keeps something (reputation, lessons).
_Novelty_: Bankruptcy is roguelike death; "I failed but learned" becomes a mechanic.

**[Mashup #23]**: Procedural Runs
_Core Loop_: Each run has a different market, investors and global events (chip crisis, new EU AI law, billionaire competitor).
_Novelty_: Roguelike variety applied to business scenarios.

**[Run #28]**: Startup Phases as Acts
_Core Loop_: Garage → Seed → Series A → Scale-up → IPO/Big Tech → Singularity; each phase changes rules and number scale; run length is emergent.
_Novelty_: Variable run length is natural — startups die at any moment.

**[Meta #52]**: The Tech Encyclopedia
_Core Loop_: Every event, character, ending and buzzword encountered unlocks a satirical-Wikipedia entry, enriched on each encounter.
_Novelty_: Completionism as ecosystem knowledge; container for more satire.

**[Meta #53]**: Satirical Achievements
_Core Loop_: Absurd badges ("Raised $1B without a product", "Your model won a Grammy", "Fired everyone but the intern").
_Novelty_: Achievements as jokes and play-style hints.

**[Meta #54]**: Unlockable Worlds as Scenarios
_Core Loop_: Startup in Europe (hyper-regulated), 1999 (dot-com bubble), Mars (billionaire in control) — each with own rules, events, satire.
_Novelty_: Prestige as world exploration rather than multipliers.

**[Meta #55]**: Unlocks = Variety, Not Power
_Core Loop_: Between runs, unlock new content (events, characters, tree branches, worlds), not numeric bonuses.
_Novelty_: Slay the Spire approach keeps balance under control; serves both run-lovers and completionists.

**[Ref #72]**: Legends Mode (from Dwarf Fortress)
_Core Loop_: After a run, browse the full company chronicle: arrivals, departures, crises, key decisions.
_Novelty_: Every run becomes a rereadable, shareable chronicle.

**[Name #79]**: Loop #N
_Core Loop_: Each run is "Loop #N"; in some endings the AI comments "Here we go again. Loop #14."
_Novelty_: The title justifies the roguelike narratively — does the AI remember?

**[End #90]**: Echoes Across Runs
_Core Loop_: A run's outcome affects later worlds as content only (billionaire got your AI → stronger rival AI next run).
_Novelty_: Narrative continuity between loops without power creep.

**[Loop #27]**: The Board Meeting as Boss Fight
_Core Loop_: Heavy decisions arrive as board meetings/funding rounds; investors have personalities and demands; outcome depends on how you played.

**[Ref #71]**: Emergent Stories (from Dwarf Fortress)
_Core Loop_: Traits and relationships generate stories: engineers fall in love, one breaks from crunch, the intern finds a vuln and blackmails you.

**[Ref #74]**: Betrayal (from EVE Online)
_Core Loop_: Investors, partners and co-founders can stab you in the back, with signals to catch it in time.
_Novelty_: Single-player distrust via characters with their own agendas.

**[Economy #96]**: Investors as Characters

### Loops, Idle & Rhythm

**[Idle #24]**: The Company Never Sleeps
_Core Loop_: Close the tab and the company keeps running; on return, a "while you were away" report with emails, tickets, updated charts.
_Novelty_: Returning feels like opening Slack after vacation.

**[Loop #26]**: 80/20 Rhythm — Triage and Board Meetings
_Core Loop_: 80% quick one-or-two-click decisions (approve, reject, assign, reply); occasionally the game pauses for a heavy decision (round, pivot, scandal, risky release).
_Novelty_: Frantic triage alternating with weighty solemn moments.

**[What If #31]**: Crises Reach You
_Core Loop_: Browser notifications when something big happens ("🚨 Your model just sued a customer").
_Novelty_: Idle isn't passive — the game calls you like a company calls its CEO.

**[Ref #75]**: From One Ship to an Empire (from X4)
_Core Loop_: Do everything by hand at first, then automate piece by piece until you manage managers of managers.
_Novelty_: Micro-to-macro arc; pairs with AI delegation (#25).

**[Ref #76]**: Living Economy (from X4)
_Core Loop_: Simply-simulated competitors (rival startups, big tech) grow, fail and acquire each other without you.

## Themes and Patterns

1. **The webapp constraint is the game** — humor, feel and twist all flow through a workspace UI.
2. **Growth vs control** — delegating to the AI always pays more, until it doesn't; this carries the title.
3. **Interlocking systems** — resources → deaths → encyclopedia → future runs.
4. **Satire of reality with unstable tone** — a serious sim in a mad world, random tonal weather.
5. **Runs produce stories** — chronicle, PDF, front page, Loop #N.
6. **Feasibility & longevity** — text, numbers and CSS; content in data files; open source; community-fed seasons.

## Promising Combinations

- **#78 + #92 + #93** — the corrupting title is the *only* visible alignment clue until you hire a Safety Researcher.
- **#25 + #24 + #15** — idle policies are Skynet's hidden engine: whoever delegates most wins faster and loses worse.
